Frequently asked questions

How much is 1 CNY in JPY today?

As of 2026-11-20, 1 CNY equals about 23.88 JPY at the mid-market rate. The exact amount you receive when exchanging money will differ slightly because providers add fees.

Why is my bank's CNY/JPY rate different?

The rate shown here is the mid-market rate — the midpoint between what buyers and sellers pay on global currency markets. Banks, cards and transfer services typically add a 1–4% margin, so your actual rate will be a little worse.
