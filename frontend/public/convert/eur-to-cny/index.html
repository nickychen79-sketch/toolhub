Frequently asked questions

How much is 1 EUR in CNY today?

As of 2026-11-22, 1 EUR equals about 7.34 CNY at the mid-market rate. The exact amount you receive when exchanging money will differ slightly because providers add fees.

Why is my bank's EUR/CNY rate different?

The rate shown here is the mid-market rate — the midpoint between what buyers and sellers pay on global currency markets. Banks, cards and transfer services typically add a 1–4% margin, so your actual rate will be a little worse.
